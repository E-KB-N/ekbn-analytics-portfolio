# Unfiltered & Unboxed

Welcome! If you stumbled across this page through my portfolio, welcome to the space where I step away from structured resume bullet points and just write.

I created this blog to share ideas without trying to fit into a single box. You'll find a mix of topics I genuinely enjoy working on, thinking about, or exploring day-to-day:

* **Sports & Tactical Analytics:** Deep dives into football match sequences, tactical breakdowns, expected goals (xG) models, and performance metrics.
* **Tech & Data Engineering:** Real-world lessons from building SQL/PostgreSQL databases, Python pipelines, BI dashboards, and evaluating AI models.
* **Career & Personal Projects:** Honest reflections on navigating the data space, learning computer science, building products in public, and growing professionally.
* **Life, Fitness & Random Notes:** Workout milestones, morning routines, competitive Scrabble tactics, or thoughts on books and media.

No rigid publishing schedules or strict categories, just genuine writing whenever inspiration hits. 

Thanks for stopping by, and stay tuned for the next post!
